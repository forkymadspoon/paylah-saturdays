“Near Me” — demo

Proof of concept for per-merchant distance sorting and the map view.

This is a demo with 8 merchants only, not the full 15,153. They are deliberately spread across Singapore (Simei, Pasir Ris, Choa Chu Kang, Woodlands, Ang Mo Kio, Tanjong Pagar), so wherever you are, the nearest one may still be some distance away — that is expected here. Distances are straight-line, not walking.
